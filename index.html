Before you open it

Your copy isn't signed by Apple or Microsoft, so your computer will hesitate once. Here's how to let it in.

  1. 1
    Open the downloaded The Voss Observatory.dmg and drag the observatory onto Applications.
  2. 2

  3. 3
    Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway beside The Voss Observatory. Confirm with your password. You only do this once.
  4. 4
    Choose Begin, then sign the guest book with your name and the code on your letter.
